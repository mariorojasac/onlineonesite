Questions

How fast can my site be live?

Starter sites go live in about 5 business days and Business sites in about 7, counted from when we have your deposit and your info (logo, photos, a short questionnaire). Rush delivery in 48–72 hours is available.

Do I have to write anything?

No. We write the copy for you from a short questionnaire, and you approve or change it before anything goes live.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages and features you need, so we give you a fixed quote upfront after a quick conversation. Call (317) 809-7843 or request a free quote below.

What do I pay up front?

50% to start and 50% when you approve the finished site, before it goes live. The free sample homepage costs nothing.

Do I own my website?

Yes, it’s your business’s website. Most clients keep it hosted with us on a Care Plan so it stays updated and secure. If you’d rather host it yourself, we’ll hand over all the files.

Can you fix my existing website instead?

Yes. If your current site is slow, outdated or not mobile-friendly, we can redesign it and keep your domain.

Will I show up on Google?

Every package includes the basics Google looks for: page titles, descriptions, a sitemap and a fast mobile site. Business and Premium also set up your Google Business Profile, which is what shows on Google Maps. Nobody can honestly guarantee the #1 spot, but these are the things that move you up.
